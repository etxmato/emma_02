How do I play the Visicom COM-100 built-in games?

This section provides brief instructions for the built-in games.

The descriptions below assume you are using a numeric keypad and have selected Settings / Use Numeric Keypad. If this option is not selected, the alternative key is shown in parentheses. For Player B, the keys W, A, S, Z control direction and TAB is used to fire. These keys work both with and without the Use Numeric Keypad setting enabled.

Doodle

Press 1 to start. A red dot will appear in the lower left corner.

Controls

Arrow Keys / Numpad 1-9   move and draw
SPACE / Numpad 0          change colour
Numpad 5 (TAB)            change colour (reverse order)

Pressing two move keys simultaneously moves diagonally. Colours cycle from red to dark green (erase), to blue, to green, and back to red.

Bowling

Press 2 to start. Players take turns.

Controls

Player A
--------
Up Arrow      hook up
SPACE         straight
Down Arrow    hook down

Player B
--------
Numpad 8 (W)      hook up
Numpad 5 (TAB)    straight
Numpad 2 (Z)      hook down

The game consists of 10 frames.

Patterns

Press 3 to start. A red dot will appear in the lower left corner. Form a simple design as in Doodle.

When your design is ready, press 3 again to repeat the pattern across the screen.

Freeway

Press 4 to start. Press SPACE to start driving.

Controls

Up Arrow      increase speed
Down Arrow    brake
Left Arrow    left
Right Arrow   right

The score is shown after 2 minutes.

Addition

Press 7 to start.

Add the digits shown and press the correct key. The faster you respond, the more points you score.

Controls

Player 1
--------
Keyboard 0-9

Player 2
--------
Numpad 0-9 (P, Q, W, E, R, T, Y, U, I, O)

The game consists of 20 numbers.
