How do I play the Studio II built-in games?

The sections below provide brief instructions on how to play the built-in games.

The descriptions below assume you are using a numeric keypad and have selected Settings/Use Numeric Keypad. If this option is not selected, the alternative key is shown in parentheses. For Player B, the keys W, A, S, Z control direction and TAB is used to fire. These keys work both with and without the Use Numeric Keypad setting enabled.

Doodle

Press 1 to start.

Controls

Arrow Keys / Numpad 1-9   move dot
Numpad 5 (TAB)            start drawing
SPACE / Numpad 0          erase

A white dot will appear in the lower left corner. Move the dot using the Arrow Keys or Numpad keys 1-9. Pressing two move keys simultaneously moves the dot diagonally across the screen.

By combining the functions of Doodle and Pattern, you can reverse the image, making your Doodle turn from a white drawing on a black background to a black drawing on a white background. Or, make just part of the screen white. You can even start another pattern and have the Studio II print over your Doodle.

After finishing your Doodle, press 2. To reverse the image, first press the Right Arrow key. Then press SPACE or Numpad 0 and the screen will start to 'paint' itself white. You can freeze the picture any time by pressing Numpad 5 (TAB).

Patterns

Press 2 to start.

Controls

Arrow Keys / Numpad 1-9   draw base pattern
SPACE / Numpad 0          generate pattern
Numpad 5 (TAB)            freeze pattern

No dot will appear until a key is pressed. Form a simple design using the Arrow Keys (or Numpad 1-9). To erase part of your design, simply trace back over it.

When your design is ready, press SPACE or Numpad 0. The Studio II will repeat your design across the screen to form the pattern.

To 'freeze' the pattern, press Numpad 5 (TAB). Restart with SPACE or Numpad 0.

After 130 key entries, the Studio II will start forming a pattern even if no SPACE is pressed.

Bowling

Press 3 to start. Players take turns rolling the ball.

Controls

Player A
--------
Up Arrow      hook up
SPACE         straight
Down Arrow    hook down

Player B
--------
Numpad 8 (W)      hook up
Numpad 5 (TAB)    straight
Numpad 2 (Z)      hook down

The game consists of 10 frames.

Freeway

Press 4 to start.

Controls

SPACE         start car
Up Arrow      increase speed
Down Arrow    brake
Left Arrow    steer left
Right Arrow   steer right
TAB           difficult start

The score is given after 2 minutes.

Addition

Press 5 to start.

Controls

Player 1      keyboard keys 0-9
Player 2      Numpad 0-9 (P Q W E R T Y U I O)

Add the digits shown in the middle and press the appropriate key. The quicker you answer, the more points you receive. The game lasts for 20 numbers.
