How do I play the MPT-02 cartridge games?

This section includes short instructions for all included MPT-02 cartridge files.

The descriptions below assume you are using a numeric keypad and have selected Settings/Use Numeric Keypad. If this option is not selected, the alternative key is shown in parentheses. For Player B, the keys W, A, S, Z control direction and TAB is used to fire. These keys work both with and without the Use Numeric Keypad setting enabled.

For Studio II cartridges that will also run on the RCA Studio III, Conic, or MPT-02, see the How do I play the Studio II cartridge games? section.

Bingo

Press one of the following keys: 

0     Stop automatic mode
1     Manual mode, then press 1 again to get a new number
2     Automatic mode, new numbers will be displayed every 12 seconds
3     Verify mode
4     Play bingo tune

In verify mode, type all winning bingo card numbers using Player B keyboard. If a number was called, 'OK' will show, if it was not called, 'NO' will show.

Concentration match

Press 0 to 9 for Player A or Numpad 0 to 9 (P, Q, W, E, R, T, Y, U, I, O) for Player B to start. Whichever keyboard is pressed first, that player starts the game. Key 0 will not show any of the symbols, key 9 will show them longest. When the game is started press two keys to select cards to be shown, a score of one point is given if two identical symbols are found.

Keys are mapped to the cards as follows:

1 2 3 Numpad 1 (Q) Numpad 2 (W) Numpad 3 (E)
4 5 6 Numpad 4 (R) Numpad 5 (T) Numpad 6 (Y)
7 8 9 Numpad 7 (U) Numpad 8 (I) Numpad 9 (O)

Grand Pack

Doodle

Press 1 to start. A white dot will appear in the lower left corner. Move the dot using the Arrow Keys or Numpad 1-9. Pressing two move keys simultaneously moves diagonally.

Press Numpad 5 (TAB) to begin drawing. Press SPACE or Numpad 0 to erase.

Press the following to change colour: 

3     Red
4     Blue
5     Violet
6     Green
7     Yellow
8     Light blue
9     White
0     Black

Patterns

Press 2 to start.

Controls

Arrow Keys / Numpad 1-9   draw base pattern
SPACE / Numpad 0          generate pattern
Numpad 5 (TAB)            freeze pattern

No dot will appear until a key is pressed. Form a simple design using the Arrow Keys (or Numpad 1-9). To erase part of your design, simply trace back over it.

When your design is ready, press SPACE or Numpad 0. The system will repeat your design across the screen to form the pattern.

To 'freeze' the pattern, press Numpad 5 (TAB). Restart with SPACE or Numpad 0.

After 130 key entries, the system will start forming a pattern even if no SPACE is pressed.

Press the following to change colour: 

3     Red
4     Blue
5     Violet
6     Green
7     Yellow
8     Light blue
9     White
0     Black

Bowling

Press 3 to start. Players take turns rolling the ball.

Controls

Player A
--------
Up Arrow      hook up
SPACE         straight
Down Arrow    hook down

Player B
--------
Numpad 8 (W)      hook up
Numpad 5 (TAB)    straight
Numpad 2 (Z)      hook down

The game consists of 10 frames.

Blackjack

Press 4 for a one-player game or 5 for a two-player game.

For all numerical input, Player A uses keyboard 0-9 and Player B uses Numpad 0-9 (P, Q, W, E, R, T, Y, U, I, O). For a one-player game the controls for Player B are used.

Controls

Player A
--------
1                 hit
2                 double
SPACE             stand

Player B
--------
Numpad 1 (Q)      hit
Numpad 2 (W)      double
Numpad 0 (TAB)    stand

When "Cut" appears on the screen press: 

1 player game     Numpad 0 (TAB)
2 player game     SPACE or Numpad 0 (TAB)

Each player bets from $1 to $9. Stakes exceeding $200 - $999 break the bank limit.

Pinball

To start press 1 for a one player game or 2 for a two player game.

Controls

Player A
--------
1                 put a ball in play
Left Arrow        left flipper
Right Arrow       right flipper
SPACE             reverse ball direction

Player B
--------
Numpad 1 (Q)      put a ball in play
Numpad 4 (A)      left flipper
Numpad 6 (S)      right flipper
Numpad 0 (TAB)    reverse ball direction

In a one player game Player B controls are used. Using TAB or SPACE too often will cause a 'TILT'.

Star Wars

Press one of the following keys: 

1     One player game
2     Two player game
3     Advanced one player game

The question "SPEED 1 2 3?" will appear on the screen, press 1 for slow, 2 for medium or 3 for high speed action. In the one player games you will use the keys for Player A. In the two player game you may use either keyboard for speed selection. The keyboard you select determines who starts chasing.

Controls

Player A (being chased)
-----------------------
Up Arrow          up
Down Arrow        down
Left Arrow        left
Right Arrow       right

Player B (being chased)
-----------------------
Numpad 8 (W)      up
Numpad 2 (Z)      down
Numpad 4 (A)      left
Numpad 6 (S)      right

If your ship is the pursuer (your ship is the viewfinder) the above keys will switch direction (up/down, left/right).
